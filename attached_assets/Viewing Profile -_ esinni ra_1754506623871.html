What is their greatest fear? Dying alone. Esinni knows that there are many ways to die in this cruel world she lives in, and her greatest fear is that she will die alone, and not in the primitive I-do-not-want-to-die-without-a-partner-or-child, but in the more sinister way of dying by herself without anyone noticing. Dying of exhaustion after falling far behind the khalasar with no one looking back; being captured in a raid and left for dead by her captors after they've tortured her, dying without anyone knowing. Esinni also fears dying without seeing her homeland ever again. She has found a new home amongst the khalasar, but she does hope to see her homeland once again. It is highly doubtful that she would leave the khalasar to return to the Isles - she has spent almost as much time away from the Islands as she did on the Islands - but she would love to see her home again. What is the most beautiful thing they've seen? It had been storming all night, to the point that Esinni, her mother, and sisters were not able to return home from the Temple. The priests and priestesses had set the women and children up for the night in the Temple. Esinni had woken before the rest of her family though, and she had made her way up one of the towers of the Temple. She looked out one of the windows - the rain had stopped, and the sun was slowly rising. There was a thick mist rolling over the grounds of the Temple, amongst the garden and other towers. The early morning sky was slowly brightening; in the distance it was a dark blue, almost to the point of still being black, but as she looked closer the sky changed. The dark blue lightened through different shades all the way to cerulean. When she looked to the sky in the other direction it was a pinkish color, the color of her mother's favorite fruit. The sun rose in the distance, yawning brightly into the world with peeking rays of sunlight over the tree-line and through the towers of the Temple. It was a beautiful morning. Esinni jumped when one of the priestesses called her name, not having heard her approaching up the stairs. Esinni took another look out the window, committing the image to memory before running to grasp the priestesses' hand as the woman led her back to her family. What is their most prized possession? This is difficult to answer, as she has two possessions that remind her of home; a pair of earrings gifted to her by her mother and a vial of perfume that reminds her of her favorite place in Koj.

The earrings are a golden color, thin, that dangle from her lobes. They are a looped metal, thin, with feathers from one of the birds native to the Islands running along the bottom. As mentioned above, they are a gift from her mother. Her mother rarely wore them, but Esinni did love when her mother wore them, believing they accentuated her skin tone and eyes perfectly. It is funny how dangly bits of metal can make accentuate a person's beauty, no?

The vial of perfume she owns reminds her of the rich scent of her home, specifically the garden the lay at the center of one of the temples. Just smelling the perfume takes her back to her childhood, when she would walk amongst the temple, the sound of birds and monkeys calling to each other from overhead. The heady smell of native wildflowers growing amongst the soil. The earthy scent of the trees with moss growing after a strong rain. Esinni was born to the Ra family on the Summer Isle of Jhala to a shipbuilder and merchant. Her father, Xharrabol, hailed from the Isle of Koj, where he trained under the master shipbuilders of the Islands. Her mother, Xhosaza, came from Jhala, the child of a merchant who had to split his business with her three siblings. She had a good childhood, her parents wrapping her in loving arms, her siblings watching her protectively. Raised on the Summer Isles, where love flows freely from person, Esinni was taught what healthy love looks like in many different ways. She spent what free time she had amongst the temples, learning the faith of her people. When not in the temples she was at the docks, watching her father's shipwrights building under his commanding gaze or spending time in her mother's office, humming as the woman reviewed the books for the trading company.
the missing girl
When Esinni turned thirteen her witchery abilities first surfaced. Her mother took note, and began subtly training her daughter in the ways to hide her abilities. Xhosaza came from a long line of Witches of the Green Hell. Her mother was a witch, as was her mother, and so on until the beginning of their line. She had seen her eldest daughter, Charanta, exhibit the same abilities, and knew the signs to watch for - a bully coming down with a sudden illness, or some poor soul that had angered the child would die from a freak accident. Xhosaza subtly trained Esinni for a year for their magic was not healing, not beneficial. It was a harming magic, a defensive or protective magic, and one whose practitioners would work during the night.

Just after Esinni's fourteenth birthday, when Xhosaza was going to introduce the girl into the family coven, the girl was kidnapped. She was taken by slavers, men who knew of the rumors of the Love Temples of the Summer Isles and their training. Men who took the girl to Yunkai, so that she could finish the training they assumed she had started. She spent the next couple of years finishing the training of a bedslave before being given to Khal Davarqo as one of many slaves.
the defender
By the time Davarqo's and Erro's khalasars met in battle Esinni had given birth to three children; a son named Villo, a daughter named Vitti, and a son named Astollo. One was the progeny of a bloodrider who used Esinni as though she were property, the next was the child of a herder and tent-mender who loved Esinni but knew he could not have her, and the third was the child of a Bravos hired by Davarqo.

When the two khalasars met in battle Esinni snuck around camp with her children, trying to escape to a hiding place to keep her children safe. There was a large yurt on the other side of camp from the battle. Esinni hid her children beneath the furs found in the yurt. She had passed a dead man on her way to the yurt and had stopped to retrieve his sword - not that she knew what to do with it. Some other women and children came into the tent, and a couple even came armed.

It was here the Esinni and Halah met. Halah entered the yurt and Esinni stormed her, swinging her sword viciously, as she knew that the woman was not of their khalasar. Of course Halah blocked her attack and disarmed her with ease. Halah and Esinni were able to down five men before the battle was over, and upon the finish of the battle Halah escorted Esinni, the women, and the children into the fold of Erro's khalasar.
the free
Esinni embraced the freedom given to her by Erro. She longed to return home, to take her children to the islands she was raised on. But she knew that she would not make it back. So she chose to stay with Erro's khalasar. The father of her second child, Marillo, chose to stay with the khalasar as well. He welcomed her into his tent, and the two had a loving relationship. Esinni's eldest child's father died in the battle, and the youngest child's father had disappeared during the fight, so the two were free to pursue a relationship at last.

Their relationship was full of love. He loved her other children, including the one that she gave birth to shortly after joining Erro's khalasar. Esinni was welcomed as one of Malah's handmaids, and she started serving her mistress faithfully. After Marillo left the khalasar, citing his ailing mother near the Skahazadhan and a younger brother in another camp. They argued and fought, but Esinni did not beg. She would not beg for love. So he left, and Esinni threw herself into serving her mistress.
the handmaid
Esinni has served her mistress faithfully since being welcomed into the fold. Esinni has become a close friends with Halah, Allayi, and Mavelli. White the three handmaids do not have defined roles that Esinni knows of, she sees differences in how the three serve Halah. Esinni sees Allayi as Halah's heart, offering her the tenderness that the khaleesi. Mavelli serves as Halah's mind, ever the calculating advice-giver. And Esinni, she is Halah's spirit, the one that exhibits her anger or frustration. All four watch over over and protect each other with a fierceness. All three of the handmaids serve their mistress faithfully in their own ways.

Esinni has taken on a more protective role. She can enter the places with Halah that men cannot, a secret blade hidden amongst her skirt. She watched as Halah trained with the Khas Losho, sitting nearby with the other handmaids. Then, in what downtime she had she would mimic their movements, their strikes. She wished to be able to defend her children if the need arose. It was one of those moments, when she mimicking the training, that Halah found her. The next day, Halah had Losho train Esinni. And so she did train, initially with sword, but more recently she has found a particular liking for archery and an interest in wielding spears.
the oblivious
Since she has become Halah's handmaid, Esinni has been training with her mistress and the Khas, Losho. The man is silent towards her for the most part, only speaking during their training to correct her form, or chastise her for simple mistakes. He does not praise her unless she makes major improvements or does something that truly impresses him.

She enjoys his silent companionship, and could see herself becoming friends with the man. Esinni has no idea of his affection towards her, her heart coldened as a result of Marillo leaving her. She has not been open to love, focusing instead on her children, her mistress, and her friends. But perhaps it is time for a change?
